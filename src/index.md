I am Andrew Zhai, currently a Ph.D. student in [Environmental Policy](https://nicholas.duke.edu/academics/doctoral-programs/university-program-environmental-policy-upep) (Economics track) at the [Nicholas School of the Environment](https://nicholas.duke.edu/) and [Sanford School of Public Policy](https://sanford.duke.edu/), Duke University. I strive to monetize the social costs (returns) of biodiversity losses (gains), particularly how ecological disturbances, such as wildlife diseases and biological invasions, affect human well-being by integrating microeconomic theory, advanced econometric and geospatial techniques, and innovative datasets.
